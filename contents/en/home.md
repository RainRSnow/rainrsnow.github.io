I am pursuing a Ph.D. at the Graduate School of Information Science and Technology, Hokkaido University. My research focuses on a RISC-V-based SoC framework for audio signal processing, including voice recognition and anomaly detection.

My experience spans digital IC design, FPGA development, and embedded systems, from RTL design and verification to timing analysis, hardware debugging, and system integration. Earlier projects in FPGA-based sound source localization and brain-computer interfaces laid the foundation for my work in hardware-software co-design.
